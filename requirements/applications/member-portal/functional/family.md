# Family

This document covers the family information a signed-in member can view in the member portal.

## Status

Working draft. Confirm which family details each member may see or update.

### MEM-FAM-001 — View family information

**Actor:** Signed-in members linked to a family.

**Requirement:** A member can view the family information and relationships they are permitted to access.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- The portal identifies the member’s family and the family members visible to them.
- Relationship details are presented in a clear and respectful way.
- A member cannot view another family’s information.
- Missing family information is handled as an empty or unavailable state, not as another family’s data.

### MEM-FAM-002 — Request a family information change

**Actor:** Signed-in members.

**Requirement:** A member can identify how to request a correction when family information is incomplete or incorrect.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- The portal provides a clear next step for requesting a correction.
- The portal does not imply a change was saved unless the member has an approved editing capability.
